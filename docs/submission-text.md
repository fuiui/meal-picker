## Title

What to Eat

## Short description（14 词）

A simple meal picker helping students choose what to eat by budget and taste.

## Long description（4 段）

What to Eat is a small website for students who find it difficult to decide what to eat. It helps them explore meal ideas while keeping a budget and taste preference in mind. The website includes a homepage, a meal picker, a collection of twelve example meals, and an about page.

Users can choose any budget or a limit of 15 or 25 yuan, then select a spicy, non-spicy, or unrestricted taste preference. The picker filters the menu first and randomly selects a matching meal. It explains when no meal matches, clears old results when preferences change, and resets choices when the page is refreshed. Random selections can repeat.

The website uses HTML for its structure, a shared CSS stylesheet for responsive layouts, and plain JavaScript for its menu and interactions. The collection and picker use the same data and card-building function. The design uses a cream background, dark green text, and orange-red accents. Local SVG illustrations indicate food types rather than the exact appearance of individual dishes.

This project was developed with Codex assistance, including planning, code, illustrations, and draft wording. Prices and meal descriptions are examples created for the coursework, not live restaurant information. The site does not offer accounts, ordering, or saved preferences. The student is responsible for reviewing the work and explaining the design and implementation.
